ℹ️ Instructions

Welcome to KryloCanvas (Web-WhiteBoard).

  • Universal Engine: Your color and thickness settings dynamically update the Pen, Pencil, Highlighter, and inserted Shapes in real-time.
  • Select Tool: Unlock and select, move, scale, rotate, and delete drawings, shapes, and imported images.
  • Solid Rulers & Snapping: Instruments act as mathematically solid boundaries. Trace edges with the pen/pencil to create perfectly straight or curved snapped lines that won't bleed through.
  • Precision Compass: Fix the needle center, extend arm infinitely to scale (in cm, mm, and inches), and draw exact mathematical arcs & circles with live scale readout and stylus snapping.
  • Page Styles & Themes: Switch between Blank (no grid), Standard Grid, Single Lines (Ruled), Dotted, and 5 Millimeter Graph colors (Green, Blue, Red, Orange, B&W) with automatic color inversion.
  • Flowcharts & Trees: Insert horizontal process flows, vertical hierarchy trees, or custom branching diagrams. Customize colors and themes per block, double-click or use the in-page editor to write text, and add/remove subordinate leaf nodes via interactive HUD controls or pull/push handles.
  • Custom Controls: Select an instrument or image to reveal the red 'Delete' icon (top right) and blue 'Rotate' arrows (top center).
  • Text Tool: Pick Text, tap anywhere on a page and type plain text. Tap existing text to edit it; use Select to move or resize it.
  • Preview Tool (hand): Pinch in/out (or Ctrl + scroll on desktop) to zoom the whiteboard pages, and drag to move around. Pinching on the top/side menus zooms the menus only.
  • Image/PDF Import: Native integration for importing images to the canvas and multi-page PDF generation.

🧭 Precision Compass Instructions

Draw mathematically exact arcs and circles to scale without error, matching the whiteboard ruler:

📍
1. Fixed Needle (Pivot)

The red crosshair marks the needle center. Drag the compass with Select tool to place the pivot anywhere on any page.

📏
2. Extend Arm Infinitely

Adjust the radius in cm, mm, or inches via the slider or type an exact measurement. The arm can be infinitely extended across any page without limits.

⭕
3. Draw Arc & Circle

Click "Circle" for a full 360° circle or "Arc" for an exact arc, rendered with your universal color and thickness.

🧲
4. Mathematical Snapping

Drawing near the compass with Pen, Pencil, or Highlighter automatically snaps onto the exact circumference!
